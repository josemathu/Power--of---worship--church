ABOUT US

Who We Are

Power of Worship Church is a family of believers called to worship God, grow in His Word, pray together and make a difference in our community.

We believe in the transforming power of Jesus Christ and His ability to give new life, purpose and hope.

Our Vision

Raising ministers with integrity to serve in the Body of Christ.

Ephesians 4:11

Our Pastor

Pastor Margaret

OUR MINISTRIES

Growing Together in Christ

Every ministry has a purpose in building faith, serving others and glorifying God.

👧

Children's Ministry

Raising a generation for Christ.

Our Teaching

We help children discover God's love, learn His Word, develop a life of prayer and worship, and grow in Christian character.

“Train up a child in the way he should go...”
Proverbs 22:6

👥

Youth Ministry

Empowering young lives.

Our Teaching

We equip young people to grow in Christ, make godly choices, discover their gifts and become positive examples in their generation.

“Let no one despise your youth...”
1 Timothy 4:12

👨

Men's Ministry

Raising Godly men.

Our Teaching

We encourage men to grow in faith, integrity and responsibility, lead their families well, serve God faithfully and positively impact their communities.

“As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.”
Joshua 24:15

👩

Women's Ministry

Encouraging and supporting women.

Our Teaching

We create a Christ-centred environment where women grow spiritually, encourage one another, discover their gifts and serve God with purpose.

“She opens her mouth with wisdom...”
Proverbs 31:26

🎵

Worship Ministry

Lifting His name in praise.

Our Teaching

We lead God's people into meaningful worship through praise, music and a lifestyle that honours God and points hearts toward His presence.

“The true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth.”
John 4:23

🌍

Evangelism & Missions

Reaching the lost locally and globally.

Our Teaching

We share the Gospel of Jesus Christ and demonstrate God's love by reaching people, families and communities with the message of salvation and hope.

“Go therefore and make disciples of all nations...”
Matthew 28:19

🙏

Prayer Ministry

Praying for lives, families and nations.

Our Teaching

We build a strong culture of prayer and intercession, standing in faith for individuals, families, the church, our community and nations.

“Pray without ceasing.”
1 Thessalonians 5:17
